THE PLATFORM

A disciplined path from market observation to bounded action.

DOMINUS is designed as a layered research and execution-control environment. Market data and external context are evaluated as evidence; decisions remain paper-level until separate economic, execution and governance conditions justify a later promotion stage.

01

Market Observation

Chronological public-market observation with a focus on point-in-time integrity, replayability and measurable data quality.

02

Deterministic Decisioning

Deterministic decision logic is used to make research outputs reproducible and to preserve clear boundaries between evidence and action.

03

Evidence Governance

Research evidence, chronology and decision provenance are retained so conclusions can be examined without granting execution authority.

04

Contextual Intelligence

Experimental research layers evaluate broader market state and external-event context without becoming an authenticated trading channel.

05

Staged Promotion

Research, forward paper evaluation, live shadowing and later bounded execution are treated as distinct stages rather than a single jump to live capital.

06

Execution Control

Authenticated execution is isolated behind explicit operational and risk controls; research outputs do not directly submit orders.

DESIGN PRINCIPLES

Authority is earned, not inherited.

≠

Signal ≠ order

A model output, event hypothesis or market regime is evidence. It is not an executable instruction.

Ø

NO_TRADE is first-class

Insufficient evidence, degraded conditions or ambiguous state can resolve to no action instead of forced participation.

↺

Promotion is reversible

Research or execution status can be demoted when evidence quality, calibration, operating conditions or validation identity deteriorate.

⊙

Evidence stays contextual

Large datasets are evaluated with recency, regime coverage and dependence in mind rather than treating raw row count as proof.

EVIDENCE DISCIPLINE

Technical correctness and economic evidence are different questions.

DOMINUS deliberately separates deterministic reference testing, exact-source technical validation, chronological out-of-sample evidence, paper evaluation and later execution evidence. A passing technical test does not imply profitability or live authorization.
